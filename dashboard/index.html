What it is

One place to see and trade MINIMA wherever it trades: Dexicon, PandaPools, MEXC and XT.com. It shows every order book side by side, finds where the venues' prices cross, rehearses a market maker on paper, and places real trades, one press at a time, inside limits you write down first.

It is made for people who already run their own Minima node and know how exchange API keys work. It runs on your own computer and talks only to your node and to the venues.

How to use it

  1. Connections. Start your node (its own wallet, funded only with what it may lose) and let it reach the chain tip. Connect the MEXC and XT.com keys you want to use. The Dexicon relays are set already.
  2. Settings, Mode. Paper to rehearse: the paper maker trades against real prices, nothing reaches a venue. Live to trade for real.
  3. Market maker, Envelope and ladder. In live mode, write an envelope: what may be traded, the size per trade, trades a day, the loss cap. It cannot be changed once written; new limits mean a new run.
  4. Settings, Automatic trading. Arm it only if the envelope turns on take when crossed or a taker rule. Your own trades do not need it. It is always off after a restart.
  5. Trade. Pick the venue at the top. The candles (1 minute to 1 week), the order book of every venue (combined or one at a time, keys 1 to 5) and the ticket side by side. Click a price in the book to use it. Click a level in the book to fill in its price and everything up to it. With quick orders on (bottom of the ticket), a right-click on the chart places an order of that size at that price, waiting on the book: above the mid a sell, below a buy (live mode, inside the envelope). Click an order line to modify it, or its x to cancel it. Pick a venue, Buy or Sell, Market or Limit, preview, then send: each send is one real trade. Underneath: your open orders ("By hand" or "App"), balances, the desk's trades and its state.
  6. Overview. Follow the venues, the routes between them and the desk's result.

Turn the ? marks on or off in Settings, Appearance: each explains the setting beside it.

Safety

  • Exchange keys: give them reading and spot trading only, never withdrawal or transfer, and limit them to your computer's IP address. They are stored encrypted for your Windows user, outside the app's folder, and never shown again. Never paste a secret anywhere else.
  • The bot's wallet: its own node and wallet, never your main one. Fund it only with what you can afford to lose, and back up its seed from the node's own terminal before funding it. This app never reads the seed.
  • Limits: every real trade happens inside the envelope's caps. The desk stops itself on any unclear outcome, on a half-done route, on the loss cap, or after a restart, and waits for you to look and release it.
  • Nothing trades on its own unless you turn on "take when crossed" or a taker rule in an envelope and arm the desk, in live mode.
  • Check every trade where it happened, on the exchange or on chain, not only here.
  • Keep it local: the app listens on this computer only. Do not expose it to a network.
